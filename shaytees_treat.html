From Cart to Crown 👑

Shaytee's Treat started with a simple dream — to bring premium ice cream and delicious treats to the students and families of Akure. What began as a humble cart near FUTA Southgate has grown into Akure's One & Only Ice Cream Shop.

Today, we serve hundreds of happy customers daily from our two locations at FUTA Southgate, offering everything from creamy gelato-style ice cream to savory noodles, crispy waffles, and refreshing coffee.
